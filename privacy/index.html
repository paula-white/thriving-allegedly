Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Thriving, allegedly. is a daily affirmation app. We keep things simple: there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking across other apps or websites. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.

What stays on your phone

Your topics, reminder settings, poster style, saved favourites and which affirmations you've seen are stored only on your device. Delete the app and they're gone.

What we collect

To understand how the app is used and make it better, the app sends us a small amount of anonymous information:

  • A random ID created when you install the app. It isn't linked to you, your Apple ID or your device's advertising identifier.
  • What you do in the app, such as opening it, revealing, saving, sharing or downloading an affirmation, and finishing setup.
  • Your app settings: chosen topics, reminder frequency and time, whether notifications are allowed, and your poster style.
  • The app version, and whether it was installed from the App Store or TestFlight.

If you choose to send feedback or rate an affirmation, we also receive what you write or tap, along with your phone model and iOS version so we can fix problems. Please don't include personal details in feedback.

How we use it

Only to improve the app: for example, which affirmations people love, which settings are popular, and what isn't working. We don't sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it to identify you.

Where it's stored

This information is stored securely with our database provider, Supabase. Only we can read it.

Permissions

  • Photos: only used to save posters you choose to download. We never read your photo library.
  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled on your phone. We don't send them from a server.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

Because the information is anonymous, we can't link it to you. If you'd like us to delete feedback you've sent, or have any questions, contact us at hello@thrivingallegedly.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
